How to play Infinite Alchemy

Start with five elements and combine them into anything. The AI decides what you made and paints it.

Start playingSee starter recipes

The five starters

Every game begins with Earth, Water, Air, Fire and Time.

EarthWaterAirFireTime

Combine elements

Drag one element onto another on the workbench, or tap one and then another. You can mix two, three or four elements at once: hold your element over a second one for a moment to gather it, then drop onto a third.

Water+Fire=Steam

Each new result joins your library on the shelf, so you can keep combining. Search the library, pin favorites, and clear or tidy the workbench any time (with undo).

Discover something new

The first time anyone in the world tries a combination, the AI decides the result and it is saved for everyone. If nobody has ever made what you just made, it's yours: you earn a first discovery seal.

Everyday combinations give the answer you'd expect. Clever, specific combinations can land on famous people, places, myths, characters and brands.

Rarity

Rarity comes from depth: how many rounds of combining an element is from the starters. Elements go from common to uncommon, rare, epic and legendary as you go deeper.

Daily challenges

Every day there are three targets to make: easy, hard and expert. They change at midnight on your device's local date and can always be made with two-element recipes that already exist. Complete at least one a day to keep your streak going. See today's challenge.

Saving your progress

Progress saves automatically in your browser. To continue on another device, open Settings in the game and use the transfer code. If you played the original version in the same browser, your elements and challenge history are restored on your first visit.

Questions

Is Infinite Alchemy free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no account, download or ads.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It's designed for touch first: drag from the shelf, or tap to combine. It also works with a mouse and keyboard.

Are results the same for everyone?

Yes. The first time anyone tries a combination, the AI decides the result and it is saved for everyone, forever.

Is this Little Alchemy?

No. Infinite Alchemy is an independent game inspired by Little Alchemy. Instead of a fixed list of recipes, an AI invents and paints new elements as you play.
